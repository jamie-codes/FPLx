- Pill labels: "Captain" | "Transfer"
- Season summary line 1: `Total transfer regret: {N}pts across {N} GWs`
- Season summary line 2: `Engine better: {N} | You better: {N} | Tied: {N}`
- Delta engine-better: `+{N}pts (engine better)` (text-red-600 dark:text-red-400)
- Delta user-better: `−{N}pts (good hold)` (text-green-600 dark:text-green-400) — use MINUS SIGN char `−` (U+2212), NOT ASCII hyphen
- Delta tied: `0pts (tied)`
- Delta null: `—` (EM DASH U+2014)
- Engine no-snapshot: `No model snapshot` (italic, text-zinc-400 dark:text-zinc-500)
- Held: `Held — no transfer` (plain, not italic)
- Loading: `Loading transfer history…`
- Empty: `No transfer history yet — data accumulates each GW after this version is deployed.`
- Error: `Failed to load transfer history. Check your connection and refresh.`
- aria-label on toggle wrapper: `Backtester view`
- aria-label on chart wrapper: `Transfer regret per gameweek`
